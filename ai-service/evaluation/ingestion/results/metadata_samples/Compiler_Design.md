## Course Objectives :

- a. Introduce the fundamental concepts and phases of compiler design, including lexical, syntax, and semantic analysis.
- b. Explain parsing techniques, such as top-down and bottom-up approaches, and their role in syntax analysis.
- c. Teach syntax-directed translation, intermediate code generation, and explore runtime structures like symbol tables and memory management.
- d. Provide an overview of web and IP security principles to support the development of secure systems.

| Module - 1 | No. of hours |
|--------------------------|----------------|
| Introduction to Compiler | 9 |

Definition of compiler, interpreter and its differences, the phases of a compiler , role of lexical analyzer, regular expressions, finite automata, finite automata to regular expressions, bootstrapping, Lexical Analyzer Generator.

| Module - 2 | No. of hours |
|--------------------|----------------|
| Parsing Techniques | 9 |

Context free grammar, derivations, parse trees, ambiguity, eliminating ambiguity, elimination of left recursion, left factoring, top down parsing: backtracking, recursive descent parsing, predictive parsers, LL(1) grammars, bottom up parsing: LR parsers, Simple LR, Canonical LR(CLR) and Look Ahead LR (LALR) parsers, Operator precedence parsing.

| Module - 3 | No. of hours |
|-----------------------------|----------------|
| Syntax-Directed Translation | 9 |

Syntax directed definition, construction of syntax trees, S-attributed and L-attributed definitions, Syntax-directed Translation schemes and applications, Intermediate code, postfix notation, Parse trees &amp; syntax trees, three address code, quadruple &amp; triples, Translation of simple statements and control flow statements.

## Module - 4

## Symbol Tables &amp; Run-Time Environments

No. of hours

9

Symbol Tables: Data structure for symbols tables, representing scope information. Run-Time Environments: Implementation of simple stack allocation scheme. Error Detection &amp; Recovery: Lexical Phase errors, syntactic phase errors and semantic errors.

## Module - 5

## Code Optimization &amp; Generation

## No. of hours

9

Design Issues, the Target Language. Addresses in the Target Code, Basic Blocks and Flow Graphs, Optimization of Basic Blocks, Code Generator. Code optimization: Machine-Independent Optimizations, Loop optimization, DAG representation of basic blocks, value numbers and algebraic laws, Data-Flow analysis: constant propagation, liveness analysis. Note: Developing a simple parser using Lex/Yacc will be given as a part of assignment.
