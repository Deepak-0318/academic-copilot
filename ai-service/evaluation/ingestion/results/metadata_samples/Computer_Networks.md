## Course Objectives :

- a) Learn about distance-based classification of networks and various mobile communication technologies in the Networking domain
- b) Analyze the system requirements of the Internet and its design parameters for supporting different types of applications
- c) Demonstrate the role of Spanning Tree Protocol in removing loops within a LAN
- d) Understand core Internet protocols (TCP, UDP) and the principles behind VLAN, VPN, NAT, and VoIP.

## Course outline (Syllabus of the course)

## Syllabus:

## Module - 1: Brief History and Introduction

No. of hours

6

Brief history of Internet and its evolution; difference between asynchronous and synchronous communication with RS-232 and I2C examples; client-server and peer-to-peer models; distance-based classification of networks, such as PAN, LAN, MAN, WAN, SAN; Introduction to Internet access infrastructure in India. Introduction to the mobile communication technology evolution from 1G to 5G. Node devices, connecting devices and Network Devices(Router, Switch, Bridge, Hub, Gateways)

| Module - 2: Layered Architecture | No. of hours |

System requirements of the Internet; network performance parameters, such as bandwidth, latency, delay, jitter, network byte order, generic network messages and headers format, layered architecture, interfaces between the layers and services provided and also introduction to 7 layers of OSI model, TCP/IP Protocol suite.

Ethernet standard 802.3, CSMA/CD, Ethernet frame structure, MAC address format, wireless communications 802.11 frame structure, Wi-Fi, ISM, Bluetooth and piconet.; Three types of switching technologies, datagrams, virtual circuits, source routing.

## Module - 4: Addressing Schemes

No. of hours

8

Internetwork, Internet Protocol (IP), IP service model, IP datagram delivery model; IPv4 header format, need for fragmentation and reassembly and its implementation. Introduction and motivation for IPv6. IPv4 addressing schemes: classful and classless addressing schemes, classful addressing schemes, class A, B, C, D and E, Address Resolution Protocol (ARP); Dynamic Host Configuration Protocol (DHCP), Internet Control Message Protocol (ICMP). Network administration tools, ping, tracert. Motivation behind subnetting and classless addressing.
