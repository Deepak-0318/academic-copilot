Course Overview The course "Engineering Explorations" is designed to provide a comprehensive introduction to engineering visualization, manufacturing techniques, and emerging technologies in engineering. The syllabus begins with the fundamentals of engineering drawing, including BIS conventions, freehand sketching, and an overview of Computer-Aided Drafting (CAD) software. The course covers orthographic projections of points, lines, planes, and solids using first-angle projection methods to develop spatial reasoning. Isometric projection techniques are taught to represent three-dimensional objects accurately. An introduction to Additive Manufacturing (AM) familiarizes students with 3D printing processes, materials, and post-processing methods. Practical sessions include modeling and printing geometric shapes like triangles, pentagons, and hexagons. Students also engage in interdisciplinary 2D drafting and 3D printing projects.

## Module -I

6 Hrs

## Introduction to Engineering Visualisation

Importance of Engineering drawing, BIS Conventions of Engineering Drawing, Freehand sketching of engineering drawing, Scales (Only introduction, Not for CIE &amp; SEE). Introduction to Computer Aided Drafting software, Coordinate system and reference planes HP, VP, RPP &amp; LPP of 2D/3D environment. Selection of drawing sheet size and scale.

## Orthographic Projections of Points and Lines

Introduction to Orthographic projections: Orthographic projections of points in 1st and 3rd quadrants. Orthographic projections of lines (Placed in First quadrant only).

## Orthographic Projections of Planes

Orthographic projections of planes viz triangle, square, rectangle, pentagon, hexagon, and circular laminae (Placed in First quadrant only using change of position method).

## Module - II

## Orthographic Projections of Solids:

Orthographic projection of right regular solids (Solids Resting on HP only): Prisms &amp; Pyramids (triangle, square, rectangle, pentagon, hexagon, cube).
