## Course Outcomes:

- a) Understand binary number systems, codes, and error detection/correction techniques in digital systems.
- b) Apply Boolean algebra to simplify logic expressions and design circuits using logic gates and standard forms.
- c) Analyze and design combinational and sequential circuits such as adders, multiplexers, and counters for practical applications.
- d) Evaluate the role and functionality of memory and programmable logic devices like RAM, ROM, and PLDs in digital systems.
