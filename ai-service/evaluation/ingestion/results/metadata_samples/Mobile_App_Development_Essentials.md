## Course Objectives :

- a) To introduce students to the mobile application ecosystem and app development lifecycle, including no-code, low-code, and traditional development approaches.
- b) To enable students to design and build functional mobile applications using no-code and low-code platforms.
- c) To provide guided exposure to Android Studio and real mobile app project structure through template-based and assisted development.
- d) To develop UI/UX design skills, product thinking, and basic knowledge of testing and deployment of mobile applications.
