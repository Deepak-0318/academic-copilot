## Course Objectives :

- a) To introduce foundational concepts in set theory including notation, operations, and representations.
- b) To develop analytical skills in understanding and applying relations and functions.
- c) To enhance the ability to model logical structures using combinatorics and pigeonhole principle.
- d) To provide essential mathematical tools for applications in computer science and data structures.

## Module - 2: Set Operations and Venn Diagrams

No. of hours

3

Operations on sets: Union, Intersection, Difference, Complement, Symmetric Difference Properties and laws of set operations, Venn diagrams, Cartesian product of sets.

## Module - 3: Relations

No. of hours

3

Definition of relation, Types of relations: Reflexive, Symmetric, Transitive, Equivalence, Representation of relations, Matrix and digraph representation.

## Module - 4: Functions and Their Properties

No. of hours

3

Definition of functions, One-to-one and onto functions, Function composition, Invertibility and inverse of functions, Pigeonhole Principle.

## Module - 5: Permutations and Combinations

No. of hours

3

Rule of Sum and Product, Permutations and Combinations, Binomial theorem, Combination with repetition.

## Course outcomes

- a) Understand and apply basic concepts and operations of set theory using set notation and Venn diagrams.
- b) Analyse and represent different types of relations using matrices and digraphs.
- c) Demonstrate the ability to work with functions, their compositions, inverses, and apply the pigeonhole principle.
- d) Apply basic counting techniques and solve problems involving permutations, combinations, and binomial expansion.
