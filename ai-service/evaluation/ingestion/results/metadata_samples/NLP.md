## Course Objectives:

Introduce students to the foundational concepts of linguistic structures and classical Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques.

Develop students' ability to design and train deep learning models for solving real-world language processing problems.

Empower students with advanced skills in leveraging transformer-based architectures and transfer learning for cutting-edge NLP tasks.

Provide experiential learning opportunities through practical

implementation of speech-enabled, multilingual NLP systems using state-of-the-art open-source tools.

## Module - 1 Foundations of NLP &amp; Embeddings No. of hours 6

Text Preprocessing: Tokenization, Lemmatization, Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging, Named Entity Recognition (NER).

Language Modelling: N-Grams, Perplexity.

Traditional Representations: Bag-of-Words (BoW), Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF).

Modern Embeddings: Word2Vec, Global Vectors for Word Representation (GloVe), FastText.

Contextual Embeddings: Embeddings from Language Models (ELMo), Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers (BERT) embeddings.

## Module - 2 Deep Learning for NLP No. of hours 6

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for Text.

Sequence Models: Recurrent Neural Network (RNN), Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU), Attention Mechanism.

Evaluation Metrics: Bilingual Evaluation Understudy (BLEU), Recall-Oriented Understudy for Gisting Evaluation (ROUGE), F1, Precision, Recall.

Use Case: Text classification (news, emails, etc.).

## Module - 3 Transformers &amp; Foundation Models No. of hours 6

Transformer Architecture.

Pretrained Models: BERT, Robustly Optimized BERT Pretraining Approach (RoBERTa), A Lite BERT (ALBERT).

Generative Models: Generative Pretrained Transformer (GPT-2, GPT-3), Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer (T5).

Fine-tuning and Transfer Learning.

HuggingFace Transformers Library.

## Module - 4 Advanced Applications in NLP No. of hours 6

Question Answering (QA) Systems: Closed-domain QA and Open-domain QA.

Conversational AI: Dialogflow (by Google), Open-source Conversational Framework (Rasa). Summarization: Extractive and Abstractive.

Machine Translation: OpenNMT, MarianMT.

Real-World Case Studies: Healthcare, Finance, Legal NLP, etc.

## Module - 5 Speech and Multimodal NLP No. of hours 6

Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) using Wav2Vec 2.0 (by Facebook AI), Whisper (by OpenAI).

Text-to-Speech (TTS) with Tacotron 2 and FastSpeech 2.

Multimodal NLP: Contrastive Language-Image Pretraining (CLIP) - OpenAI, Flamingo (DeepMind), Large Language and Vision Assistant (LLaVA).

NLP for Low-Resource Languages Overview - Multilingual BERT (mBERT), Cross-lingual RoBERTa (XLM-R), IndicNLP.

## Course outcomes

- a) Apply foundational and contextual NLP techniques using linguistic preprocessing, word embeddings, and classical models.
- b) Design and implement deep learning architectures for sequence modelling and text classification tasks.
- c) Analyze, fine-tune, and deploy transformer-based models for advanced NLP tasks: sentiment analysis, question answering, and summarization.

- d) Develop and integrate real-world NLP applications including speech-based systems, machine translation, and dialogue systems.
